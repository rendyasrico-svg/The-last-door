HOW TO PLAY

Use the left joystick to move.

Swipe the right side of the screen to look around.

Press CROUCH to move quietly — The Caretaker hears you less.

Press INTERACT to open doors, pick up items, and solve puzzles.

Find the key, unlock the cabinet, and unlock the front door to escape.

Find weapons to fight back: a melee weapon (INTERACT when close) and a pistol (SHOOT button, limited ammo).

Don't let The Caretaker see or hear you. If it catches you, it's game over.
